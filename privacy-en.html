Somacast — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Summary

Somacast works without an account. Everything you enter (height, weight, blood pressure, conditions, habits, questionnaire answers, diary entries) is stored only on your device. We do not receive, store on servers, sell or share this data. The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

Who we are

The developer of the Somacast app ("we"). Privacy contact: somacast.app@gmail.com.

What data is processed and where

Health and lifestyle data you enter yourself: sex, age, height, weight, blood pressure, heart rate, cholesterol, glucose, waist circumference, conditions, family history, smoking, alcohol, sleep, activity, answers to the PHQ-9, GAD-7 and AUDIT-C questionnaires, checkup dates, habits and goals. It is kept in the app's local storage on your device and used only for calculations and display inside the app.

Settings (language, theme, reminders) — also on the device only.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs.

Purchases

The Premium subscription is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive your card details. The app only checks on the device whether a subscription is active. Payment processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, the system may store the app's data in your Google account. You control this in Android settings; we have no access to those backups.

"Save backup to file" creates a file only where you choose. The file contains your health data and document photos — keep it safe.

If you turn on "Auto-copy", the app updates a file you choose (for example, on Google Drive) after every change. The file is stored where you chose and handled by that service under its own terms; we have no access to it. You can turn auto-copy off in settings.

Document recognition

When you scan a photo of a medical record or outpatient card, the text is recognized on your phone (Tesseract library), without internet. The photo and text are not sent anywhere; only what you confirm is added to your card.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect (Android 14+), the app only reads steps, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, weight and blood pressure for recent days — only after you grant permission and when you ask it to. This data goes into the diary and profile on your device, is not shared with anyone and is not used for advertising. The app does not write anything to Health Connect. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Medicines, symptoms and widget

Your medicine list, dose check-offs, pain entries and questions for the doctor are stored only on your device. The home-screen widget shows the card name, health index and today's reminders — anyone who sees your screen can see them; you can remove the widget at any time.

SOS card

If you turn on the SOS card on the lock screen, your blood type, allergies, conditions, medicines and the contact you entered are shown in a notification visible without unlocking the phone. This is intentional, so paramedics can see them; turn the card off in the app if you don't want this.

Reminders and doctor report

Reminders are generated on the device. The PDF report is created on the device and shared only when and with whom you decide.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process children's data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete everything in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app (and removing any Android backup in your Google settings).

Your rights

Because we do not receive your data, you fully control it on your device: you can view, correct, export and delete it yourself. For any personal-data questions (including under the GDPR and Ukrainian data protection law), contact us at the address above.

Medical disclaimer

Somacast is an informational app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis and treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
